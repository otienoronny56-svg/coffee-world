Our Genesis

Coffee World Investments Limited was born from a simple belief: Kenyan coffee is among the finest in the world, but the farmers who grow it and the global roasters who brew it deserve a more transparent bridge.

We serve as a transparent, direct bridge between our partner farmers and the global market. Our role is to ensure that when an importer in Europe, a coffee lover, or a roaster in any part of the world tastes our coffee, they are experiencing the unique, natural flavors from our high-altitude farms.

While Kenya remains our primary heart, our expertise has expanded to identifying and sourcing unique lots from across the East African coffee belt—including the bright berries of Ethiopia, the complex beans of Rwanda, and the powerful profiles of Uganda.

A Master Barista crafting our signature roasted profiles.

The High Altitudes

While we source directly from select cooperatives across Nyeri, Kirinyaga, and Murang'a, our footprint extends to the rolling hills of Kiambu, the fertile plateaus of Uasin Gishu, the lush tea-adjacent hills of Kericho, and the high-altitude slopes of Mt Elgon.

Growing at altitudes above 1,700 meters across these diverse Kenyan regions, our Arabica beans mature slowly in the rich volcanic soil, developing the bright acidity and deep taste that define our coffee.
